What this is

Conditions for Curiosity is a podcast and a companion app, both built by one person — me, Kevin. The app is a tool to help you have curiosity-building conversations with your child; it's not a diagnostic tool, it doesn't assess your child, and it's not a substitute for advice from a teacher, pediatrician, or other professional who actually knows your family.

Who can use it

The app is meant to be used by you — a parent or caregiver — not by your child directly. You should be old enough, wherever you live, to agree to terms like these yourself. There's no account system or login; your device holds your place in the app, tied to a random session id, as described in the privacy policy.
