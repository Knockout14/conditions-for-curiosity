# .ux-profile.md — read by the VectorLab UX skills

**Source of truth: `DESIGN.md`.** Read it before changing layout, color, type, or motion. Where a
VectorLab rule and `DESIGN.md` disagree, the exceptions below are deliberate brand decisions —
don't "fix" them. Everything not listed here: follow the VectorLab rule.

## Product & aesthetic

- Editorial podcast site + small mobile companion app. Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no framework.
- Aesthetic reference: print editorial / literary magazine at night — not SaaS, not Linear/Stripe.
  Restrained: navy ground, one gold accent, serif display + sans UI.
- Pages are reading surfaces, not product chrome. Most VectorLab rules about toolbars, tables,
  dashboards, and drawers rarely apply here.

## Documented exceptions

### Spacing
- **Type-derived spacing is allowed** for vertical rhythm around text (e.g. 22px, 26px, 38px
  margins, 11px cadence padding). The 8pt grid applies to new components, gutters, and padding.
- **Measure:** body capped at 62ch, lead at 56ch, hero sub at 50ch. Narrower than the 65–75ch rule
  on purpose — serif-led editorial column. Do not widen.

### Typography
- **Two families:** Playfair Display (display/headlines) + Karla (UI/body). Allowed.
- **Body line-height 1.72** (site) and 1.68 (app) — light text on near-black needs more leading. Allowed.
- **Uppercase tracked labels at 11px** (eyebrows, tags) are an allowed exception to the 12px floor —
  caps + .14–.24em tracking reads larger than mixed-case 11px. Floor for these is **11px**.
  Anything below 11px is a defect (see "Adopted" below).
- Display letter-spacing −.01 to −.015em is fine (Playfair is not a variable cut here).

### Motion
- **Hero entrance `.reveal`: 780ms, `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1)`, ~60ms stagger.** One-time, hero only,
  editorial pacing. Allowed exception to the 300ms ceiling. Not to be reused for UI chrome.
- **Hover durations 140–200ms** — round new ones to 150 or 200ms tokens.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is mandatory everywhere (see DESIGN.md).

## Adopted from VectorLab (applied 2026-10-01)

- Nothing below 11px — all 10/10.5px text raised to 11px (site, app, model).
- Animate transform/opacity only — episode-row hover now uses `transform: translateX(10px)` on the
  number and title instead of animating `padding-left`.
- Duration tokens — existing 140/160ms hovers rounded to 150ms. New transitions: 100/150/200/300ms.
- Max 3 Karla weights — body moved from 300 to 400; Karla now loads 400/500/600 only.
  A stray `font-weight:700` in the app (never loaded) is now 600.
